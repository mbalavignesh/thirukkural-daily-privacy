Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026

In short: Thirukkural Daily does not collect, store, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking, and it works fully offline.

Who we are

Thirukkural Daily (“the app”) is an independent Android app that presents the couplets of Thiruvalluvar’s Thirukkural with English translations, meanings, and short parables. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not ask you to sign in or create an account, and it does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files.

All 1,330 Kurals, translations, and stories are included inside the app. Reading them does not send anything over the internet.

Information stored on your device

The app saves one setting on your device: the background texture you last chose in Share Studio. It stays on your phone, is never sent to us or anyone else, and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Sharing images

When you tap Share Image, the app creates a picture of the Kural on your device and opens Android’s standard share menu. The image goes only to the app or person you choose, such as WhatsApp, Instagram, or your messages. We never receive a copy. After that, the receiving app’s own privacy policy applies.

Advertising, analytics, and third parties

  • The app shows no advertisements.
  • The app uses no analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking services.
  • The app does not use the Android advertising ID.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any information with third parties, because we have none to share.

Children’s privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your choices

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing to access, correct, export, or delete on our side. To remove the one setting stored on your phone, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android Settings.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how information is handled, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.
